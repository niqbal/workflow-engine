wfe.yaml as in docs/MULTI-MACHINE.md: node, listen {api, internal}, advertise, peers, store {kind, url_env}, cluster_key_env, num_shards, max_clock_skew. Secrets only from the environment. The cluster key becomes Config.TokenKey on every node. Start-up refuses: num_shards different from the store's, a node missing from peers, memstore with more than one peer, a missing or short cluster key when peers > 1. Without --config, wfe server keeps WI-025's single-node memstore behavior. Wires httptransport (WI-053) as the node's Transport.

Dependencies: WI-025, WI-053, WI-062. All of them are already merged on main: items are moved to factory/ready only after their dependencies merge, so do not ask about them.
Spec: docs/SPEC.md. Do not edit existing tests; contract tests are owned by the human.
